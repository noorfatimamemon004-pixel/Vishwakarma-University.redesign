Product Design

Shape the future of innovative products by learning the art and science of product design. Master how to conceptualize, prototype, and launch products that delight users and solve real-world problems.

Duration 4 Years
Credits 120 ECTS
Mode Offline
alt="Product Design" >

Core Modules

A comprehensive curriculum designed to give you the skills needed to excel in Product Design.

💡

Sketching

Learn fundamental concepts of design principles to understand problems, develop and ideate innovative product solutions.

Key Topics
Drawing Basics Visual Thinking Product Forms Ideation
🎨

Rendering

Build prototypes and learn how to communicate your product before launch.

Key Topics
Rendering Material Exploration 3D Visualisation Design Systems
🔍

User Research

Conduct research to understand user needs, behaviours and pain points to inform product decisions.

Key Topics
Observations Usability Testing Analysis Persona Development
⚡

Interaction Design

Design intuitive and engaging interactions for digital and physical products.

Key Topics
Interaction Principles Physical Design Motion Design Accessibility
🧩

Product Ergonomics

Master the fundamentals of human-centred design, usability, physical comfort and safety.

Key Topics
Ergonomics Safety Factors Layouts Human Factors
💻

Software

Gain practical skills in design software and digital tools to develop your product ideas.

Key Topics
3D Modelling Rendering Tools Prototyping Digital Drafting

Graduation Project

In your final year, you complete a comprehensive graduation project that demonstrates your mastery of Product Design, from initial research through to final delivery and testing.

  • End-to-end project from research to delivery
  • Portfolio-ready case study
