Product design

Innovate solutions that blend form, function, and sustainability

At Vishwakarma University, you will learn to blend creativity with functionality in Product Design, creating innovative, user-focused solutions.

What can you pursue further with this specialization:

  • Product / Industrial Designer
  • Sustainable Product Designer
  • Furniture & Space Designer
  • Smart Systems & IoT Product Designer
  • CMF (Color, Material, Finish) Specialist
  • Design Consultant in Manufacturing & Infrastructure
