UI/UX Design

Shape the future of digital experiences through human-centered design. Master the art and science of creating intuitive, beautiful interfaces that delight users and solve real-world problems.

Duration 4 Years
Credits 120 ECTS
Mode Offline

Core Modules

A comprehensive curriculum designed to give you the skills needed to excel in UI/UX design.

💡

Design Thinking

Master the design thinking methodology to tackle complex problems through empathy, ideation and iteration.

Key Topics
Empathy Mapping User Interviews Problem Framing Ideation
🎨

Prototyping

Learn to bring your ideas to life through low-fidelity sketches to high-fidelity interactive prototypes.

Key Topics
Wireframing High-Fidelity Prototypes Micro Interactions Design Systems
🔍

User Research

Develop expertise in qualitative and quantitative research methods, learn to conduct interviews and usability tests.

Key Topics
Qualitative Research Usability Testing Analytics Persona Development
⚡

Interaction Design

Study the principles of how users interact with digital products. Design intuitive navigation and meaningful interactions.

Key Topics
Information Architecture Navigation Design Motion Design Accessibility
🎭

Visual Design

Master the fundamentals of visual communication, typography, colour theory and layout.

Key Topics
Typography Colour Theory Layout & Composition Visual Hierarchy
💻

UX Softwares

Gain practical coding skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Understand tools used to create user-friendly websites.

Key Topics
HTML & CSS JavaScript Basics Prototyping Tools Design Handoff

Graduation Project

In your final year, you complete a comprehensive graduation project that demonstrates your mastery of UI/UX design, from initial research through to final delivery and testing.

  • End-to-end project from research to delivery
  • Portfolio-ready case study
