At Vishwakarma University, your degree is designed to be flexible.
Along with your core specialization, you'll have the freedom to explore Minors, Open Electives, Value education Courses and Skill Enhancement Courses — allowing you to shape your learning experience around your interests and goals.

Minor

  • 6-semester course
  • 4 credits per semester
  • Subject chosen once for the entire duration
  • Includes assignments, CIEs, and end-sem exams

Open elective

  • 1-semester course
  • 3 credits per semester
  • New subject every semester
  • Includes assignments, CIEs, and end-sem exams

Skill enhancement course

  • 3-semester course
  • 2 credits per semester
  • New subject each semester
  • Includes assignments and CIEs

Value education course

  • 4-semester course
  • 4 credits per semester
  • University-offered subjects
  • New subject each semester
