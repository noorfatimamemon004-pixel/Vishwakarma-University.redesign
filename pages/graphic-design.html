Graphic Design

Shape the visual language of tomorrow through the power of storytelling and creativity. Master the art and strategy of graphic design to craft compelling visuals that inspire, communicate ideas, and bring brands to life across every medium.

Duration 4 Years
Credits 120 ECTS
Mode Offline

Core Modules

A comprehensive curriculum designed to give you the skills needed to excel in Graphic Design.

💡

Design Fundamentals

Understand the core principles of graphic design, from composition and hierarchy to balance and contrast.

Key Topics
Basic Layout Typography Visual Hierarchy Colour Theory
🎨

Brand Identity

Learn how to craft memorable brand identities that tell a story, develop logos, palettes, and visual systems that reflect personality and purpose.

Key Topics
Positioning Brand Guidelines Symbolism Logo Design
🔍

Typography & Layout

Develop expertise in visual hierarchy, grid systems, leading and kerning, and establish clarity across different media.

Key Topics
Font Selection Layout Grids Hierarchy Editorial Design
⚡

Packaging Design

Study the process of designing packaging that communicates a brand's identity and creates engaging product experiences.

Key Topics
Packaging Structures Visual Concepts Material Design Sustainability
🎭

Illustration & Imagery

Master the fundamentals of visual communication, illustration, imagery and creative storytelling.

Key Topics
Visual Storytelling Digital Illustration Composition Image Making
💻

Motion Graphics

Master the art of type and animation. Explore how text, graphics and imagery shape communication and visual identity.

Key Topics
2D Animation Motion Principles Storyboarding Digital Media

Graduation Project

In your final year, you complete a comprehensive graduation project that demonstrates your mastery of Graphic Design, from initial research through to final delivery and testing.

  • End-to-end project from research to delivery
  • Portfolio-ready case study
