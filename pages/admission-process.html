ADMISSION PROCESS

Joining Vishwakarma University is the first step toward unlocking your potential. We've outlined a clear and easy process to help you complete your application with confidence.

1

VUNET (Written Exam)

Assess your design knowledge and aptitude

Duration: 2 hours

Multiple choice and drawing questions

Covers fundamental concepts and problem-solving

2

Studio Interview & Portfolio Round

Showcase your work to our faculty

Present your portfolio and creative work

Discussion with faculty members

Q&A session about your background and goals

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Know if you are eligible to apply for the B.Des course at Vishwakarma University.

Passed 10+2 examination.

Obtained at least 45% marks (40% in case of candidates belonging to reserved category) in the qualifying examination.

Passed D. Voc. Stream in the same or allied sector.

PROVISIONAL ADMISSION

1

Step 1

Fill up the Basic Information (Application Form) and Pay Rs. 1500/- (Non-Refundable) to apply for Provisional Admission.

2

Step 2

Fill up Detail Provisional Admission Form and Pay Rs. 25,000/- (First Installment) to secure provisional Admission.

  • 12th Examination / Equivalent Diploma Mark Sheet / Passing Certificate.

  • Graduation Passing Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate / Birth Certificate / School Leaving Certificate (anyone)
